# Expanse Tracker Mission

## Core objective

Expanse Tracker is a calm, personal finance dashboard for recording everyday spending and understanding where money goes each month. It turns small individual entries into a clear monthly picture without the complexity of a full accounting suite.

## Target audience

The primary audience is an individual managing personal or household expenses, especially someone who wants a lightweight mobile-friendly ledger for:

- Utilities and household bills
- Bazar and grocery spending
- One-time purchases

The product is designed for people who value quick entry, readable totals, and a focused overview more than advanced budgeting workflows.

## Problem it solves

Everyday expenses are easy to forget or scatter across notes, messages, and bank records. Expanse Tracker provides one simple place to:

- Add, edit, and delete expense entries
- See the total spent in a selected month
- Compare spending across Utilities, Bazar, and One-Time categories
- Review a concise recent-expense trail
- Export a monthly report for personal records

The product goal is a clearer, calmer relationship with spending: useful financial detail without visual or operational clutter.
